MoFang GO Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to MoFang GO. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Information We Collect

MoFang GO does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Government identification information

All user-generated data remains stored locally on your device.

GO Challenges

MoFang GO includes a GO Challenge feature that allows users to share challenge links through Universal Links and App Links.

GO challenge information is not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

Shared links are used solely to transfer challenge identifiers between devices.

Local Storage

The application stores information locally on your device to provide its functionality, including:

  • Application settings
  • Solve history
  • Rank progress
  • User nickname
  • Formula collections
  • Other user-generated content

This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Permissions

Camera

Camera access may be used for:

  • Scanning QR codes
  • Capturing cube sticker images
  • Capturing algorithm reference images

Photo Library

Photo Library access may be used for:

  • Selecting QR code images
  • Selecting cube sticker images
  • Selecting algorithm reference images

Images are processed locally on your device.

Internet Access

Internet access may be used for:

  • Opening shared GO links
  • Accessing the Privacy Policy and related web pages
  • Downloading publicly available application resources

MoFang GO does not use internet access to collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

MoFang GO is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. However, users of all ages may use the application.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and Analytics

MoFang GO currently does not:

  • Display advertisements
  • Use advertising identifiers
  • Use analytics services
  • Use tracking technologies
  • Share user data with third parties

Data Retention and Deletion

All user data is stored locally on the device.

Users may remove all application data at any time by:

  • Deleting the application
  • Clearing application data through the operating system

The developer does not maintain copies of user data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
