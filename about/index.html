Cubing GO

Speedcubing Timer & Challenge App

About

Cubing GO is a speedcubing timer and challenge platform designed for practice, competition, and shared solving experiences.

Philosophy

We focus on simplicity, fairness, and pure solving experience.
No distractions. Just you, the cube, and the time.

GO Challenge

The GO feature allows players to share scrambles and compete using the same challenge link across devices.

Privacy

All solve data is stored locally on your device.
We do not collect or upload personal data.
